Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common queries about Parul University.

Is there an entrance exam for admission?

Admission criteria vary. Many programs accept scores from national exams like JEE or NEET, while others have merit-based entry or personal interviews.

What types of hostel facilities are available?

We offer a variety of options including AC and Non-AC rooms, with dedicated wings for international students and premium amenities.

What kind of scholarships does the university offer?

Parul University provides merit-based scholarships for high-achieving students, as well as financial aid for sports achievers and defense personnel wards.

Can I apply for multiple courses at once?

Yes, you can apply for multiple programs. Our counselors and the Campus AI can help you determine which program best fits your career goals.
